AgenticLab

A private, single-user personal automation project. It is not a product or a service, and it is not offered to the public.

What it does

AgenticLab is a personal assistant that runs on its owner's own server. On the owner's request, or on a schedule the owner sets, it can:

  • find and read the owner's own Gmail messages, read-only, to summarise or answer questions about them;
  • read the owner's Google calendars to show a weekly planning view and remind the owner of birthdays and events;
  • keep the owner's Google Tasks lists up to date at the owner's request;
  • process files the owner sends, and produce reports, documents and charts;
  • look up public information such as weather forecasts and public company news;
  • deliver results to the owner only, through the owner's messaging and email channels.

Google user data

AgenticLab requests, for one account (its owner's): read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly) and to Google Calendar (calendar.readonly), and access to Google Tasks (tasks). It never sends, deletes or modifies email, and never changes calendar events. It is used by its owner alone, and no other person can sign in to it. The details are in the privacy policy.
